# MATERIALMIND launch identity

## Style Prompt
An open scientific instrument with a mineral-dark observation chamber and ivory research work surface. Match the functioning MATERIALMIND LAB interface. Let actual crystal geometry, prediction intervals and experiment plots carry the visual interest. Motion is a quiet camera or a precise reveal, never a substitute for measured evidence.

## Colors
- `#14252b`: mineral observation background
- `#eef0ec`: ivory work surface
- `#344d3a`: forest text
- `#76916f`: secondary scientific labels
- `#cbe1d2`: pale mint highlights on dark

## Typography
Helvetica/Arial for display and body; monospace for instrument labels and numbers. System fonts only. Large film text is at least 48px; scientific chart labels remain part of the authentic exported chart.

## What not to do
- No invented discoveries, wins, curves, or UI states.
- No generic neon, gradient spectacle, or abstract technology filler.
- No animated progress implying a live DFT calculation.
- No voice cloning, cloud narration, or unlicensed music.
