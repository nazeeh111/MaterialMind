# MATERIALMIND launch film

## Planning rubric

1. MATERIALMIND is a local scientific laboratory for ranking existing crystal structures and testing acquisition policies against prior DFT reference labels.
2. The claim worth showing is that the next evaluation can be chosen, explained, and audited. The film does not claim a new material or a benchmark win.
3. The visual hook is the ivory scientific work surface around a dark, physically faithful crystal unit cell.
4. Show the real selected crystal, its calibrated interval, a reference-label reveal, and the actual multi-seed result plot.
5. Twenty seconds is sufficient: 4 + 6 + 5 + 5 seconds.
6. Polished scientific instrument: confident holds, precise labels, and restrained movement.
7. A quiet original synthesized harmonic score, with sparse soft accents. No narration, voice identity, or external music-license assumptions.
8. Share copy: “MATERIALMIND LAB makes crystal-selection decisions inspectable, then tests them against reproducible retrospective evidence.”
9. User flow: inspect a real structure → read prediction and uncertainty → reveal a previously computed DFT reference and update → compare campaign results.

## Visual identity

Use the actual application identity: dark mineral `#14252b`, ivory `#eef0ec`, forest `#344d3a`, muted green `#76916f`, pale mint `#cbe1d2`. The original cube/atom brand mark is a diagrammatic symbol, not a simulated calculation. Helvetica/Arial display and monospace labels match the installed system type route used by the app. No cloud fonts or network assets during rendering.

## Storyboard

- **0–4 s, hook:** original MATERIALMIND brand composition, “Make the next evaluation count.” The illustrative unit-cell mark is explicitly labelled. Soft entrance, then a clean hold.
- **4–10 s, examine:** an authentic current-source lab screenshot fills the working surface. “Inspect the structure. Read the uncertainty.” Slow, subtle camera movement across the screenshot. No manufactured UI values.
- **10–15 s, evaluate:** an authentic screenshot after the actual reference reveal. “Reveal a reference. Update the model.” The caption explicitly says “Previously computed DFT · retrospective replay.” It must not suggest live DFT execution.
- **15–20 s, evidence:** the actual result figure from saved experiment outputs, with “Compare. Reproduce. Question.” The figure retains its methods, axes and confidence intervals. Footer: “Retrospective WBM study · no new DFT or synthesis.”

Crossfades connect scenes. Content remains legible and settled for most of each shot. Original synthesized audio fades in and out; no narration. Scene transitions use sparse bell-like accents synchronized to actual cuts at 4, 10, and 15 seconds. The score is compositional atmosphere, not a representation of scientific computation.

## Sources and restrictions

`docs/media/brand.svg` is original vector branding. `docs/media/lab.png` and `lab-revealed.png` must come from the functioning current application. The benchmark shot must use an actual saved quantitative figure. Do not substitute fabricated screenshots or invented curves. No private data or credentials enter the film.

## Music cue guidance

Original deterministic synthesis is generated by `make_score.py`. No beat detection is needed: the score is composed for the fixed storyboard, with restrained accents at its three transitions. Ambient motion does not encode measured energy or uncertainty.
